Town by town: what each page covers

Each town page is written for that town. They are not copies with a name swapped in, so it is worth opening yours before you call.

Wakefield sits to the north around Lake Quannapowitt. The page focuses on older housing, pipe materials and what each fails at.

Reading is the farthest of the six, on Route 28 beyond Wakefield. The page explains how we prepare for the longer run and why tree roots matter for sewer lines there.

What to tell the dispatcher about your location

Give your street, your town and a landmark. In Woburn, that might be the Common or the highway interchange. In Wakefield, Lake Quannapowitt. In Melrose, Ell Pond or the Highlands. In Winchester, the Center or the Mystic Lakes. In Reading, the Common or the river.

A landmark helps the dispatcher confirm the address on a night when a map app sends drivers the wrong way, and it saves minutes when minutes matter. If you are in Stoneham itself, a cross street near Main Street, Stoneham Center or Spot Pond does the same job.
