Repairs and emergency services

These are the services people call about when something has failed. They are covered by the 24/7 emergency line.

Drain Cleaning

Sinks, tubs, toilets and floor drains cleared by a plumber, with a 24/7 line for backups.

Hydro Jetting

High-pressure water that cleans the whole pipe wall, for lines that keep clogging or will not clear with a cable.

Sewer Line Repair

Camera-confirmed diagnosis first, then the smallest repair that solves the problem.

Sewer Line Cleaning

Main-line cleaning for backups, slow drains throughout the house, and lines that need regular maintenance.

Leak Detection

Find the leak first, open as little as possible, and repair the right pipe.

Burst Pipe Repair

Water on the floor? Shut it off, call the 24/7 line, and we repair the pipe and check for damage behind it.

Water Heater Repair

No hot water, a leaking tank, or a pilot that will not stay lit? Call the 24/7 line and a plumber will diagnose it.

Water Heater Replacement

A failed tank, replaced fast, sized for your household and installed to code.

Toilet Repair

Overflowing, running, leaking or loose, a toilet problem gets fixed once the cause is found.

Faucet Repair

Drips, leaks, low flow and stuck handles in kitchens, baths, laundry rooms and outdoor spigots.

Garbage Disposal Repair

Jammed, humming, leaking or clogged, your disposal can often be repaired or replaced.

Water Line Repair

The pipe between the street and your house, found, repaired and tested.

Commercial Plumbing

Plumbing for restaurants, shops, offices and multi-unit buildings, with a 24/7 line for failures that stop business.

Sump Pump

A sump pump that fails in a storm is a flooded basement. Repair, replacement and battery backup, on a 24/7 line.

Boiler Repair

No heat in the middle of a New England winter is an emergency. We diagnose water-side and gas-side boiler faults.

Not sure which page fits?

Start with the symptom. A drain that is slow or backed up points to drain cleaning. Water where it should not be points to leak detection or burst pipe repair. A cold shower points to water heater repair. A damp basement points to sump pump service.

If none of those fit, call anyway. The dispatcher will sort it out and send the right plumber.
