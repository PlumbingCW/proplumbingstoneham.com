Lindenwood is a residential area of Stoneham, with Lindenwood Road, Ledgewood Drive and Myopia Road among its streets and Montvale Avenue nearby. Pomeworth Field, the Tri-Community Greenway and Spot Pond are close.

The edges are not formally drawn. Maps and listings use the name a little differently, so if you are not sure your street counts, call and tell us where you are.

A mix of house styles

Real estate listing data for the area describes mostly Colonials and Capes, with some nineteenth-century homes near Main Street, twentieth-century Cape Cods and Tudor Revivals, ramblers, bi-levels and split-levels with attached garages, and a few newer Colonial Revivals. The same data puts the median year built at 1956 and the typical lot at about 10,000 square feet.

Condominium buildings sit along the borders of the area, near Montvale Avenue. If you own one, start with your association about building-wide shutoffs, then call a plumber for the fixtures and lines inside your unit.

Buying or selling in Lindenwood

A general home inspection may not include the sewer line or the water heater's remaining life, and those are two of the larger repair items in any house. Before you close, ask whether a sewer camera inspection makes sense, and ask the seller how old the water heater is.

If you are selling, a recent camera video and a note of the water heater's age are simple things to hand a buyer. Both take a few minutes to gather and they answer questions before they become negotiations.

Basements and wet weather

Many split-level and Cape-style houses have a basement or partial basement. If yours takes on water after heavy rain, look at the sump pump first. Check that it runs, that the discharge pipe leads away from the house and that a battery backup is in place if the power goes out in a storm.

Ask the Stoneham Building Division or Public Works what is allowed for a discharge line at your address.

Everyday problems we hear about from homes like these

  • A toilet that runs, rocks or clogs.
  • A slow kitchen or bath drain that needs drain cleaning.
  • A water heater that is leaking, noisy or out of hot water.
  • A sump pump that hums but does not move water.
