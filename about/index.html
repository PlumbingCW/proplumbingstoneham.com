Who we are

Stoneham Pro Plumbing is a plumbing service for Stoneham, Massachusetts. We answer one 24/7 line, send plumbers to Stoneham, Woburn, Wakefield, Melrose, Winchester and Reading, and handle both emergencies and planned work.

This site is our front door, and it is deliberately simple. There are no forms and no pop-ups. You call, a person answers, and the plumber comes. Everything on these pages is written to help you decide what to do next.

How we work

Every visit follows the same order. The plumber finds the cause, shows you what they found, quotes the price, and starts only when you agree. If a camera, a meter test or a pressure test will answer the question, we use it first.

  • Diagnose before quoting, with a camera or a test when it helps.
  • Clear price before work starts.
  • No pressure to approve more than the problem needs.
  • Written summary and video on sewer and leak work, when you want it.
  • Permits handled where the work requires one.

What we handle

We repair and install across the whole house: drains and sewer lines, water heaters and boilers, leaks and burst pipes, sump pumps and basement water, fixtures and repiping, plus plumbing for restaurants, shops and offices. The full services list has a page for each.

What you will not find here

You will not find invented reviews, star ratings or testimonials, and we do not list license numbers or named staff on this site. We would rather say less and keep it true. If you want to verify a plumber's license, the Massachusetts Board of State Examiners of Plumbers and Gas Fitters offers a lookup, and any plumber should be glad to give you their details.

What a typical emergency visit looks like

The call comes first. The dispatcher asks where the water is, what has failed and whether anyone is in danger, then tells you what to shut off. While you do that, the schedule is checked for the nearest plumber with the right equipment.

On arrival, the plumber looks before touching anything. A leaking heater gets its fittings and valves checked. A backed-up drain gets a cable and, where it helps, a camera. A wet ceiling gets a meter test and moisture readings. You are told what was found, what the fix involves and what it costs, and only then does the repair begin.

Afterward, the plumber tests the repair, cleans the work area and tells you what to watch for. If the problem points to something larger, such as an old sewer line or a pipe run near the end of its life, you hear that too, with options and no pressure.

How we think about pricing

Plumbing prices depend on the cause, the access and the parts, so we do not publish a flat menu that would be wrong half the time. Each service page lists what moves the price for that job, and every quote is given before work starts.

Night and weekend dispatch is available 24/7, and the price for that visit is stated up front like any other. If you want a second opinion, a camera video or a written summary, ask for it.

Stoneham roots, nearby reach

Stoneham is a town of roughly 24,000 people near Spot Pond and the Middlesex Fells Reservation. Plumbers who work here learn the roads, from Route 28 and Main Street to the Interstate 93 and Route 128 links, and they know the older housing stock that makes up much of the area.

That local base is what lets us reach Woburn, Wakefield, Melrose, Winchester and Reading quickly. It is also why each service page ties its advice to what we see in this part of Middlesex County.
