What to have ready when you call

A minute of preparation shortens the call and helps the dispatcher send the right plumber and equipment.

  1. Your street address and a nearby landmark or cross street.
  2. What is happening, which fixtures are affected and when it started.
  3. Whether water is still running, and whether you have found the main shutoff.
  4. For a heater or boiler, the fuel type and approximate age.
  5. A phone number the plumber can use to reach whoever will give access.
