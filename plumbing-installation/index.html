Rough-in, then finish

Plumbing happens in two stages. At rough-in, the plumber installs the pipes inside the walls and floors before drywall goes up. At the finish stage, the fixtures are set, the valves are connected, and the system is tested.

Good coordination between the plumber and the other trades keeps the job on schedule. A change after rough-in, such as moving a sink, is more costly than the same change before. Decide on fixtures and locations first, and give us the spec sheets.

  1. Walk the project and review the plans or the layout.
  2. Written scope and price, with permit needs explained.
  3. Rough-in and inspection before walls are closed.
  4. Fixture set and connection after finishes are in.
  5. Final testing and a walkthrough of the shutoffs.

Permits and licensed work in Massachusetts

Under 248 CMR, plumbing and gas fitting work in Massachusetts requires licensed professionals and, in most cases, a permit from the local inspector. In Stoneham, the Building Division issues permits for plumbing and gas work and handles the inspections. Ask it what your project needs.

We pull the permit for the work we do and arrange the inspections. The inspector checks the rough-in before the walls are closed, so do not close walls until you hear it has passed.

Older Stoneham houses and what is behind the wall

Public housing data put the typical Stoneham home at around the late 1950s. Houses of that age often have cast iron or galvanized pipes that were fine for the time but do not always match modern layouts. A remodel is a good time to replace sections that are in poor condition.

When we open a wall, we look at what is in there. If the supply pipes are corroded, you would usually rather replace them now than after the new tile is in. See our repiping page for the options.

Choosing fixtures that last

We can install fixtures you supply, and we can recommend models if you ask. A fixture with a hard-to-find part is a future repair headache, so keep the packaging and manuals.

If you are adding a disposal or a dishwasher, our garbage disposal team can install and test the unit with the rest of the sink.

  • Pick fixtures with a warranty from a company that sells parts.
  • Check the rough-in dimensions of toilets, tubs and vanities before ordering.
  • Choose shutoff valves and supply lines that are easy to reach and replace.
  • Ask about water-saving models that fit your household.
  • Keep spare parts, such as cartridges, for the faucets you install.

What affects the price of plumbing installation

Scope

A single fixture is a short job. A bathroom or kitchen rough-in is a larger one.

Access

Open framing and an unfinished basement are quick. Finished walls need more care.

Fixtures

Standard fixtures and designer fixtures differ in price and in installation time.

Venting and drains

Moving drains and adding vents is the largest cost driver in many remodels.

Permits

Permits and inspections are built into the project plan.

Planned installations are not an insurance matter. If the project follows a covered loss, ask your insurer what documentation they need.

New plumbing follows 248 CMR, the Massachusetts plumbing and gas regulations, and is inspected by the local plumbing inspector.
