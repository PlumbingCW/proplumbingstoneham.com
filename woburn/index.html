Roads and landmarks we use to find you fast

Woburn is crossed by Interstate 93 and Interstate 95 (Route 128), with Route 38 running toward Stoneham and Route 3 passing through the city's northern side. The Anderson Regional Transportation Center sits near the 128 and 93 interchange, and Woburn Common anchors the downtown area.

When you call, give the nearest cross street or a landmark such as the Common or the transportation center. It helps the dispatcher confirm the address and gives the plumber a back-up if a navigation app sends them to the wrong side of a highway.

Older homes, older pipes

Many Woburn neighborhoods have older housing, and older houses commonly have cast iron drains, galvanized supply pipes or both. These materials corrode with age, and roots find the joints in an old sewer lateral. If you live in a house that has had repeated clogs, a sewer camera inspection shows whether the cause is a pipe defect and not just a clog.

If the camera shows a failed section, a sewer line repair can be planned. If it shows buildup, hydro jetting is the better cleaning method than a cable.

Winter freezes and water that will not stop

New England winters stress pipes in garages, crawl spaces and exterior walls, and Woburn homes are no exception. A frozen pipe often splits quietly and leaks only after it thaws. If you find a wet ceiling or hear running water in a wall, shut off the main and call.

Our burst pipe repair page has the step-by-step shutoff list. The short version is to close the main valve, open a low faucet to drain the line, turn off power near the wet area and take photos.

Commercial buildings near the highways

Woburn has a large business base along the highway corridors, with office buildings, light industrial space, restaurants and shops. A grease-lined kitchen drain or a failed water heater in a commercial space shuts business down. We work after hours when possible, and the 24/7 line is open for failures that cannot wait.

For landlords with several tenants, a one-page list of shutoff valves and cleanouts is a time-saver on every call. Ask the dispatcher how to set it up.
