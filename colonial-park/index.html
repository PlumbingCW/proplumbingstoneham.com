Colonial Park is a residential neighborhood of Stoneham next to the Middlesex Fells Reservation, with Stone Zoo and Spot Pond nearby. Recreation Park, Whip Hill Park, Pomeworth Field and the Tri-Community Greenway are all close.

The name is also the name of an elementary school, Colonial Park Elementary, and Stoneham's elementary school zones are Colonial Park, Robin Hood and South. Real estate listings use the name for the surrounding neighborhood, and its edges are not formally set.

The housing in Colonial Park

Listing data describes mostly single-family homes, with Cape Cods, ranches, Colonials and some newer homes, and condominiums or multifamily units at the lower end of the price range. The same data puts the median year built at 1960 and the typical lot at about 10,450 square feet, and most streets have sidewalks.

Plumbing under a busy household

A family home asks a lot of its fixtures. Several showers in the morning, a dishwasher and laundry running at once, and a few things that should not go down a toilet will test any system. Small repairs done early cost less than the same repairs after water has spread.

Keep a basic kit at home. A plunger, a flashlight, a bucket and the phone number you will call take only a minute to set out, and every adult in the house should know where the main shutoff is.

Near the Fells

Wet basements after heavy rain or snowmelt are a fact of life in many New England houses. If you see water in the basement after a storm, check the sump pump first. If you notice a damp spot in the yard or a jump in the water bill, a leak on the service line is possible, and leak detection can locate it.
